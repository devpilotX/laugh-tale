- 8-2 (table in 8.6) - Every buy price exceeds its sell price by at least the minimum spread, checked at both extremes of the dynamic band - evidence: none named; implied price-table validation report
- 8-3 (table in 8.6) - Order-book match is atomic; killing the server mid-match creates or destroys no item and no Berry - evidence: none named; implied before/after inventory and balance diff from a kill-mid-transaction test
- 8-4 (table in 8.6) - Trade GUI resists disconnecting, swapping items after confirming, and spam-clicking - evidence: none named; implied manual exploit test log
- 8-5 (table in 8.6) - Weekly economy report generates and posts automatically - evidence: none named; implied Discord message
- 9-1 (table in 9.9) - Two hours of mining, farming and building changes RP by exactly zero - evidence: none named; implied before/after RP read
- 9-2 (table in 9.9) - Killing the same alt five times yields RP only on the diminishing pattern, then zero - evidence: none named; implied RP log across the five kills
- 9-3 (table in 9.9) - The reset runs twice in a row and produces an identical result - evidence: none named; implied standings comparison, matches Appendix B invariant four
- 9-4 (table in 9.9) - Rewards are granted before archival, verified by killing the server mid-reset and re-running - evidence: none named; implied season archive plus reward rows after re-run

- 9-7 (table in 9.9) - The Hall of Fame monument and web page both update automatically - evidence: none named; implied in-world screenshot plus web page
- Section 10 - no acceptance table exists in 10-the-rank-gated-shop.md at all, despite 10.3 stating a security-critical server-side enforcement rule ("Assume a modified client will try to buy a Tier 8 item at Tier 1 on day one"). Nothing in 8.6 or 9.9 covers it.
- Appendix B - no acceptance table, but it lists five named test invariants which are effectively criteria: killer CR never reduced by a kill; death never takes a player below their tier floor; no non-combat action changes CR; two reset applications equal one; sum of CR changes in a kill event is zero before clamping and flooring.

## CONTRADICTIONS

- MAX_GAIN is mathematically unreachable - 9.2 states "K = 24" and "Clamp: minimum +2, maximum +40 per kill", and Appendix B repeats "K = 24" with "MAX_GAIN = 40" and "raw = K * (1 - E)". Since E is strictly greater than 0, raw is strictly less than 24, so the plus-40 ceiling can never bind - either K is wrong or MAX_GAIN is wrong; a builder cannot tell whether the owner wanted upset kills to be worth up to 40 (which needs K near 40 or higher) or wanted a cap that is deliberately slack.
- Death floor: ladder bottom versus own tier floor - 9.2 says the victim's loss is "floored so a player cannot be driven below the bottom of the ladder" (one global floor) VS Appendix B "CR_victim = max(tier_floor(CR_victim), CR_victim - gain)" (per-tier floor). With per-tier floors nobody can ever be demoted a tier by losing, so the ladder becomes ratchet-only and 9.3's "The top tier should be genuinely rare" collapses over time. Only the owner can decide whether demotion is possible.
- Decay base: 1 percent of CR or 1 percent of the amount above the floor - 9.2 says "RP decays 1 percent per week above the tier floor" VS Appendix B "CR = max(tier_floor(CR), CR * (1 - DECAY_RATE))", which is 1 percent of full CR then clamped. For a CR 2000 player with a 1900 floor the two readings give a loss of 1 versus a loss of 20. Twentyfold difference in decay speed; the builder cannot pick.
- Dynamic band lower bound versus the hard floor - 8.2 states "Each item price moves within a band of plus or minus 35 percent of base" and then "Hard floor at 25 percent of base". The band already stops at 65 percent of base, so a 25 percent floor is unreachable and dead. Either the band is wider than stated or the floor number is wrong; the difference decides whether a heavily farmed item bottoms out at 65 or 25 percent of base value.
- Champion means two different things - 9.3 lists a ladder tier literally named "Champion" (7th of ten, reachable by many players every season) and 10.2 gives shop "Tier 7 Champion Unlocked at Champion", VS 9.6 "exactly one player is named Champion of LaughTail. Not a top three. Not a podium. One name" plus "A permanent title ... marking them as a past Champion". Chat prefix, tab prefix and the shop unlock message all become ambiguous. Owner must rename one of the two.
- Warlord has no shop tier - 9.3 lists ten tiers "Wanderer, Settler, Raider, Fighter, Warrior, Gladiator, Champion, Warlord, Legend, Mythic" VS 10.2 whose unlock column covers only "Everyone, Settler, Raider, Fighter, Warrior, Gladiator, Champion, Legend and Mythic". Warlord sits between Champion and Legend and is never assigned. A Warlord either keeps Tier 7 or gains Tier 8; the tier-8 contents are explicitly "Cosmetic and status only", so the choice is not cosmetically neutral for progression pacing.
- Shop tier derived from rank versus tracked independently - 10.1 "What you may buy depends on your rank" and 10.3 "Tier definitions live in config" imply the tier is a pure function of current RP, VS 10.4 keep_peak "Keep the highest tier ever reached" and drop_one "Drop exactly one tier at reset", which require a separate persisted shop-tier column that can diverge from current rank. Both cannot be true; the data model differs.
- Reset compression applied twice - 9.4 gives "RP_new = 1000 + (RP_old - 1000) * 0.35" and then says "What resets: RP, CR, and the seasonal leaderboards", VS 9.1 "Rank Points (RP) = Combat Rating (CR)" and Appendix B "RP = CR" with the compression written only against CR. If RP and CR are separately stored and both compressed, the effective factor becomes 0.1225. Builder needs to know if RP is a stored column at all.
- Champion decision ordering versus the reset transaction - 9.6 says "The top 32 by RP at the season cutoff earn a place in the Finale" and "The Season Finale decides it. One scheduled event (12.5) produces one winner", VS 9.4 "The season resets on the 1st of every month at 00:00 server time" and "Rewards are granted before the wipe, in the same transaction". The Finale cannot both run after the RP cutoff and be complete inside the 00:00 reset transaction. Owner must set when the Finale runs relative to the cutoff and the reset.
- Deaths do affect rank - 9.8 states "Kills are the only stat that feeds rank", VS 9.2 "The victim loses a symmetric amount" and Appendix B "CR_victim = max(tier_floor(CR_victim), CR_victim - gain)". Deaths reduce CR and therefore rank. This matters because 9.8 is the text shown to players on the stats screen and would be a false statement about the ranking rules.
- Rank is Elo, not kill count - 9.1 says "Rank Points (RP) = Combat Rating (CR) ... One input", VS 9.8's claim that a tracked stat ("Kills") feeds rank. Kill count is not an input to Elo; the input is per-kill rating delta. Affects how the stats screen explains rank and whether kill count is ever used as a tiebreak.
- Spawn-camping guard scope - 9.2 requires "No RP for kills within a defined radius of spawn or of a player's own respawn point", VS Appendix B's single function "zero_if_spawn_region(location)" taking one location and no player argument. The respawn-point half of the rule is absent from the authoritative maths. A per-player respawn-point check is a very different implementation and cost from a static spawn region.
- Alt farming: zero RP versus symmetric loss - 9.2 says "Same-IP and same-payment-source kills award zero RP", and Appendix B applies "zero_if_alt_or_reciprocal" to gain, which is then also used for the victim's loss, so the victim loses nothing. But the same table's stated intent is to remove the benefit, and Appendix B's invariant "The sum of CR changes in a single kill event is zero before clamping and flooring" cannot hold once any multiplier zeroes only one side or both. As written the invariant is untestable, because the multipliers are applied after the clamp, not before it.
- Champion receives "no permission" but needs one - 9.6 lists what the Champion does not receive as "no Berries, no items, no gear, no stat bonus, no permission, no shop discount", VS the same table granting "A permanent title" (chat and tab prefix) and "A crown cosmetic ... Champion-only". Both prefixes and gated cosmetics are normally implemented as permission nodes. Builder needs the owner to confirm "no permission" means no gameplay-affecting permission only.

## AMBIGUITIES

- target_berries_per_hour has no value - 8.2 says "Set target_berries_per_hour once, as the intended income of an average player doing average activities" and every price derives from it - the number itself is never given anywhere in my sections - What is target_berries_per_hour, in Berries?
- Minimum spread has no value - 8.3 requires "sell price is strictly below buy price with a minimum spread" - whether it is a flat Berry amount or a percentage, and its size, is never stated (8.4 defers only fees and tax to Section 23) - Is the minimum spread a percentage of buy price or a flat amount, and what value?
- expected_minutes_to_obtain source - 8.2 requires "the assumed minutes per item" for every sellable item and says "any item can be sold" - no method, data source, or default for unmeasured items is given - How should minutes-to-obtain be set for an item nobody has measured, and does an unpriced item still sell?
- Dynamic price mechanics unspecified - 8.2 says "Heavy selling of an item pushes its price down; scarcity lets it recover" and "Recovery is gradual, not instant" - no elasticity constant, no window length, no recovery rate per hour, no definition of heavy - What is the price adjustment per unit sold and the recovery rate per hour?
- Daily sell volume caps undefined - 8.3 requires "Cap per-player daily sell volume per item category" - the category list, the cap values, the day boundary, and the behaviour on hitting the cap are all missing - What are the item categories, the per-category daily caps, and does the sale hard-fail or partially fill?
- Balance-growth alert threshold undefined - 8.3 says "Alert automatically on any balance growing faster than a defined multiple of the expected hourly rate" - the multiple and the measurement window are never defined - What multiple of target_berries_per_hour over what window triggers the alert?
- Auction anti-snipe window undefined - 8.4 says "Bids near the end extend the auction slightly" - neither "near the end" nor "slightly" is quantified, nor is a maximum total extension - How many seconds before close triggers an extension, by how many seconds, and is there an extension cap?
- Listing slot count undefined - 8.4 requires "the same number of listing slots" for every player - the number is never given - How many auction listing slots does each player get?
- Transfer tax threshold undefined - 8.5 requires "A transfer tax above a threshold, small enough that ordinary friendly payments stay effectively free" - neither threshold nor rate is given - Above what Berry amount does the transfer tax apply, and at what rate?
- Sink target measurement undefined - 8.5 targets "roughly 60 to 80 percent of Berries created should be destroyed again over a season" and "If supply climbs three weeks running, act" - no definition of what counts as created, and no defined automatic action - Which faucets count as creation, and is the three-week rule an alert only or an automatic price change?
- Order book versus auction house liquidity - 8.4 describes both an "Auction house" with "buy-it-now plus bidding" and an "order book" that matches buy and sell orders - whether a buy-it-now listing is visible to the matching engine is never stated - Are auction listings and sell orders the same book, or two separate books?
- Order book partial fills - 8.4 requires "never allow a partially applied trade" but a matching engine normally fills a large order against several smaller ones - it is unclear whether partial fill of an order is forbidden or only partial application of a single match - Is a buy order for 64 items allowed to fill in multiple matches?
- Rank tier thresholds absent - 9.3 says "Thresholds live in config" and "Set them from the measured RP distribution after the first season, not from guesswork" - no launch-day values exist, yet the shop in 10.2 gates on tier names from day one - What RP thresholds ship for season one before any distribution has been measured?
- Tier floor function undefined - Appendix B calls tier_floor(CR) in the death path and the decay path - since 9.3 leaves thresholds unset, tier_floor is undefined, and it is unclear whether it means the floor of the tier the player currently occupies or the global bottom - What exactly does tier_floor return?
- Server time zone unstated - 9.4 fixes the reset at "the 1st of every month at 00:00 server time" and 9.5 schedules nine countdown points against it - the actual time zone is never named - Which time zone is server time?
- Decay cadence and trigger - 9.2 says decay applies "per week ... after 7 days of inactivity" and Appendix B says "Weekly decay, only after DECAY_AFTER days of inactivity" - unclear whether the first decay lands at day 7 or day 14, whether it repeats weekly indefinitely, and what resets the inactivity clock (login, or a kill) - When is the first decay tick and what action resets inactivity?
- Diminishing-returns window semantics - 9.2 and Appendix B both say the multiplier ladder resets "after 6 hours" - unclear whether the window is 6 hours since the first kill of the pair or a rolling 6 hours since the last kill - Is the 6-hour pair window fixed from the first kill or rolling from the most recent?
- Reciprocal-kill detection undefined - 9.2 requires detecting "reciprocal kill patterns between the same pair" and zeroing them - no ratio, count, or time window is given, and no rule for how it interacts with legitimate rivalry - How many alternating kills within what window count as reciprocal?
- Combat tag duration undefined - 9.2 says "disconnecting while tagged is resolved as a death" - the tag duration and what applies or refreshes it are never stated - How many seconds does the combat tag last and what refreshes it?
- Spawn no-RP radius undefined - 9.2 says "within a defined radius of spawn" - the radius value and the shape are never defined - What radius in blocks, and is it spherical or a horizontal cylinder?
- Post-respawn invulnerability duration undefined - 9.2 requires "a brief post-respawn invulnerability that ends the instant the player attacks" - the duration is never given - How many seconds of post-respawn invulnerability?
- Finale format entirely unspecified - 9.6 says "The top 32 by RP at the season cutoff" and "One scheduled event (12.5) produces one winner" and demands "a decisive sudden-death tiebreak" - bracket, rounds, gear, arena, and no-show handling are absent from my sections - What is the Finale format, and what happens if fewer than 32 players qualify or a qualifier does not attend?

- Champion when nobody qualifies - 9.6 states "exactly one player is named Champion" every season, and 9.9 requires exactly one Champion per season row - the behaviour for a season with too few or zero eligible players is never stated - Can a season row exist with no Champion, or is the season void?

## DEPENDENCIES

- Atomic order-book matching in 8.4 requires a transactional relational database with real row locking because "make every match atomic in a database transaction, and never allow a partially applied trade" cannot be honoured on flat files or YAML storage
- Shop transaction logging and the balance-growth alert in 8.3 requires the transaction ledger schema and a stable player UUID key because alerts compare balance deltas over time
- The economy audit script in 8.3 requires the complete item price table plus the full recipe graph including "smelting, crafting, uncrafting, villager trading, and any custom recipe from a datapack" because a cycle can only be found once every edge is known
- The economy audit gate in 8.3 requires a working CI pipeline because it must "Run it in CI on every price change" and "Fail the build on any positive cycle"
- Dynamic pricing in 8.2 requires per-item sales volume history because the price band moves with actual supply
- All item pricing in 8.2 requires measured expected-minutes-to-obtain data because the base formula takes minutes as its only per-item input
- The weekly economy health report in 8.5 requires a Berry supply accounting model that separates created from destroyed because the report must state "total supply, created, destroyed"
- Money sinks in 8.5 require the claim system allowance and the home slot cap from Section 15 because both sinks are defined as purchases beyond an earned allowance
- Shop tier gating in 10.3 requires the rank and CR store to be authoritative and readable inside the purchase transaction because the check "happens when money moves"
- Rank tier chat and tab prefixes in 9.3 and the Champion permanent title in 9.6 require a permissions and prefix plugin loaded before the rank plugin because prefixes are applied through it
- Elo updates in 9.2 require a PvP death handler with reliable killer attribution including projectile and pet kills because the formula needs both CR values at the instant of death
- The combat tag rule in 9.2 requires disconnect-event handling and death resolution on quit because "disconnecting while tagged is resolved as a death"
- Alt detection in 9.2 requires IP address logging and access to payment-source records because both are the stated matching keys
- The spawn no-RP region in 9.2 requires spawn coordinates and the spawn world to exist because the check is positional
- Weekly decay in 9.2 requires a per-player last-activity timestamp and a scheduled job that survives restarts because decay only starts after 7 days of inactivity
- The monthly reset in 9.4 requires a season table with a per-season completion flag because idempotency must be guarded "with a database flag on the season row, not with a timestamp comparison"
- The reset in 9.4 requires the season archive tables to exist first because standings must be written "before anything is modified"
- Champion reward granting in 9.6 requires the archive write and reward write to share one transaction, so both schemas must exist before either can be built
- The Champion advancement in 9.6 requires a loaded datapack and a working Geyser bridge because the acceptance check is Bedrock visibility
- The Champion determination in 9.6 requires the Season Finale event system from 12.5 because the ladder only qualifies the top 32
- The Hall of Fame monument in 9.7 requires the spawn build to exist and be protected from modification because entries are "carved permanently into the monument at spawn"
- The Hall of Fame page, season history, and the countdown banner in 9.7, 9.4 and 9.5 require the website and its server-data feed because all three are web surfaces
- Stats tracking in 9.8 requires playtime, block, mob, and distance counters wired before launch because "All stats persist across seasons" and cannot be backfilled
- The one-Champion guarantee in 9.9 requires a database unique constraint in the schema because it is explicitly "not by application logic alone"
- Shop tier persistence for keep_peak and drop_one in 10.4 requires a stored per-player shop tier column because both options need history that current RP does not carry

## RISKS ON A 2 vCPU / 4 GB HOST

- The nightly economy audit in 8.3 walks "the full item table plus every recipe chain and reports any cycle with positive yield". Cycle detection over the full recipe graph including datapack recipes is the heaviest compute job in my sections; run on the game box it will contend for both vCPUs. It should run off the server thread, ideally off the box entirely.
- Order-book matching in 8.4 must be deterministic, atomic, and transactional per match. Under a burst of orders this means serialised database transactions with row locks. If the matching runs on the main server thread, lock wait time lands directly in MSPT.
- Dynamic price recomputation in 8.2 on every sale, if evaluated synchronously with a database read and write per transaction, adds I/O to the main thread on the most frequent player action in an economy server.
- Logging every shop transaction to the database in 8.3, combined with the order book and auction house, is a continuous write load. On 4 GB the database and the JVM heap compete, and the never-break rule already reserves 25 percent or 768 MB outside the heap.
- Displaying all eight shop tiers with locked items greyed out rather than hidden, per 10.3, means the largest possible GUI catalogue. Every open sends full inventory packets, and per-item lore that names an unlock tier multiplies string work per open.
- The Season Finale in 9.6 puts up to 32 players in one scheduled PvP event. That is the worst concurrent-combat case on the server: entity tracking, projectiles, particles, and potion effects all in a few chunks, and it is the single moment the season is decided, so a lag spike there is unrecoverable.
- The monthly reset in 9.4 must archive every player's standings and apply the compression, with rewards granted in the same transaction. A single large transaction over the whole player table at 00:00 can stall the server; the correctness requirement to keep it one transaction makes chunking it harder.
- The weekly economy report in 8.5 needs "the distribution of balances, and the top ten balances", which is a full-table aggregation. Cheap at 50 players, but it is a synchronous scan that should not run on the main thread.
- Anti-farm checks in 9.2 run per kill: pair repeat lookup, same-IP comparison, payment-source comparison, reciprocal pattern detection, spawn region test, and respawn point test. Six lookups per death event; if any hits the database synchronously it is a main-thread stall during combat, exactly when MSPT headroom is smallest.
- The combat tag in 9.2 requires per-damage-event bookkeeping across all players in combat, the highest-frequency event class on a PvP server.
- The Hall of Fame monument at spawn in 9.7 grows one entry per month forever. If entries use text displays, item frames, armour stands, or signs in a permanently loaded spawn chunk, per-tick entity cost grows monotonically with no bound stated.
- The scoreboard countdown "inside the final day" in 9.5 means a per-second scoreboard update for every online player for 24 hours, plus title and action-bar flashes, on top of normal load.
